Rules and odds
  • Signed in to an account, your coins are bet on the server: it shuffles, deals every card and pays out. Your browser never sees the shoe or the dealer's face-down card, and every move is checked against the real hand, so the game can't be cheated.
  • Without an account, the same rules run in your browser and bet the coins saved in it.
  • A fresh 6-deck shoe is shuffled for every hand with cryptographic random numbers, so cards can't be counted.
  • Blackjack (an Ace and a 10-value card as your first two cards) pays 3 to 2, rounded down to whole coins. Other wins pay 1 to 1, so your bet comes back doubled. A tie (push) gives your bet back.
  • The dealer checks for blackjack when showing an Ace or a 10-value card, and stands on all 17s, soft 17 included.
  • Double down on any first two cards, also after splitting: your bet doubles and you get exactly one more card.
  • Split any two cards of the same value, up to 4 hands. Split Aces get one card each, and 21 on a split hand counts as 21, not blackjack.
  • Insurance costs half your bet when the dealer shows an Ace, and pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack.
  • With perfect play the house edge is about 0.45%: over a long time you keep about 99.55% of what you bet. If you leave in the middle of a hand, it waits for you to come back.
